# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior includes:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Critiquing content rigorously but respectfully ("this step is wrong because…" not "you're clueless")
- Citing evidence for factual claims about professional practice
- Gracefully accepting and applying corrections

**Unacceptable behavior includes:**
- Trolling, insulting language, personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Plagiarizing licensed content without rights
- Adding harmful, dangerous, or deceptive "skills"
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to **aniruddhaadak80@gmail.com**. All complaints are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer is obligated to respect the privacy and security of reporters.

Enforcement decisions may include: warning, temporary ban from interactions, or permanent removal, proportionate to severity.
