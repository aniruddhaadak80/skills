# Communicate roadmaps without creating promises

> Confidence-tiered updates, decision logs, and expectation resets that survive leadership churn.

**Track:** 📋 Product Management · **Domain:** Stakeholder Alignment · **Level:** intermediate · **~25 min**

**Who this is for:** Product Managers, Product Owners, Founders, Product Analysts

## When to Use This Skill

Confidence-tiered updates, decision logs, and expectation resets that survive leadership churn.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Publish on fixed cadence regardless of news quality — silence breeds rumors
2. Tag items Now/Next/Later with confidence percentages stated
3. Maintain decision log linking asks → outcomes → rationale
4. Translate between audiences: exec summary ≠ engineering detail ≠ customer view
5. Reset expectations immediately when reality shifts; late bad news compounds
6. Archive old versions; roadmap revisionism destroys credibility

## Common Pitfalls

- Dates leaked from internal docs becoming external commitments
- Roadmap decks diverging from what engineering believes
