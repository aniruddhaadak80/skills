# Communicate during incidents as a leader

> Stakeholder cadence, uncertainty honesty, and blame avoidance setting organizational tone.

**Track:** 🧭 Engineering Leadership · **Domain:** Leadership Rhythms · **Level:** advanced · **~30 min**

**Who this is for:** Engineering Managers, Tech Leads, Staff Engineers, CTOs

## When to Use This Skill

Stakeholder cadence, uncertainty honesty, and blame avoidance setting organizational tone.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Acknowledge fast even with zero root-cause information
2. Separate customer messaging from internal technical updates
3. Never speculate causes publicly before investigation confirms
4. Shield engineers from status meeting demands mid-mitigation
5. Deliver blameless framing immediately — names leak, careers burn
6. Post-mortem summary published within 72h while memory is fresh

## Common Pitfalls

- Leaders demanding ETAs engineers cannot honestly give
- First blameful reaction poisoning all future reporting
