# Brief zoonotic and bite-prevention safety routinely

> Handling precautions, PPE triggers, and incident reporting protecting staff and public health.

**Track:** 🐾 Veterinary Medicine · **Domain:** Practice Operations · **Level:** foundation · **~15 min**

**Who this is for:** Veterinarians, Vet Techs, Practice Managers, Animal Welfare Teams

## When to Use This Skill

Handling precautions, PPE triggers, and incident reporting protecting staff and public health.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Flag suspected infectious cases at booking; isolate on arrival path
2. Species-specific handling training refreshed for all new staff week one
3. PPE rules per scenario (suspect ringworm, parvo, rabies-suspect) posted visibly
4. Bite/scratch protocol: immediate wound care + report + follow-up owner notified
5. Quarterly review of incidents shaping handling-policy updates
6. Pregnant/immunocompromised staff task adjustments planned respectfully

## Common Pitfalls

- 'Friendly-looking' animals skipped restraint protocols
- Scratches logged nowhere until infection forces it
