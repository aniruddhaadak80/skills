# Qualify GxP vendors before reliance

> Risk-tiered audits, quality agreements, and change-notification clauses protecting product integrity.

**Track:** 🧬 Biotech & Pharma · **Domain:** Supplier Quality · **Level:** advanced · **~40 min**

**Who this is for:** Bioinformaticians, Lab Scientists, Regulatory Affairs, Genetic Engineers, Clinical Data Managers

## When to Use This Skill

Risk-tiered audits, quality agreements, and change-notification clauses protecting product integrity.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Tier vendors by impact on product quality/patient safety
2. Tier-1 requires on-site or remote audit against documented agenda
3. Quality agreement defines specs, change notification windows, audit rights
4. Verify certificates scope covers ACTUAL materials/services purchased
5. Performance reviewed per lot: CoA trends, deviations, complaints
6. Requalification triggered by changes, incidents, or schedule expiry

## Common Pitfalls

- Certificates accepted covering different site or grade
- Vendor changes materializing without any notification arriving
