# Periodize training around real competition calendars

> Macro-to-micro planning balancing stress and adaptation with planned recovery weeks.

**Track:** 🏆 Sports & Performance · **Domain:** Training Design · **Level:** intermediate · **~35 min**

**Who this is for:** Coaches, S&C Specialists, Athletic Trainers, Team Scientists

## When to Use This Skill

Macro-to-micro planning balancing stress and adaptation with planned recovery weeks.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Anchor to competition dates working backwards through phases
2. Define each phase's primary adaptation goal in one sentence
3. Vary volume and intensity inversely within weeks, not both up together
4. Plan deload every 3-5 weeks BEFORE fatigue forces it
5. Individualize: adjust for athletes' life stress and sleep reality
6. Review block outcomes against phase goals; carry lessons forward

## Common Pitfalls

- Copy-pasting elite programs onto developing athletes
- Competition prep starting after it should have
