# Conduct performance improvement plans fairly

> Clear criteria, genuine support, documented checkpoints protecting everyone's dignity and legal position.

**Track:** 👥 People & HR · **Domain:** Performance & Growth · **Level:** advanced · **~40 min**

**Who this is for:** HR Managers, Recruiters, People Ops, Team Leads

## When to Use This Skill

Clear criteria, genuine support, documented checkpoints protecting everyone's dignity and legal position.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Confirm PIP is genuinely performance, not personality or preemptive layoff
2. Define measurable success criteria achievable by a competent performer
3. Offer real resources: coaching, training, workload adjustment
4. Weekly documented check-ins; no surprise-ending ambushes
5. Outcome honored either direction: extension, success, or respectful exit
6. HR review of documentation BEFORE presenting anything to employee

## Common Pitfalls

- PIPs as paperwork theater preceding predetermined termination
- Criteria vague enough that success is undefinable
