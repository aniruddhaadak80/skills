# Design pricing and packaging customers understand

> Value-metric selection, tier fences, and upgrade psychology tested against real buying behavior.

**Track:** 📋 Product Management · **Domain:** Monetization · **Level:** intermediate · **~40 min**

**Who this is for:** Product Managers, Product Owners, Founders, Product Analysts

## When to Use This Skill

Value-metric selection, tier fences, and upgrade psychology tested against real buying behavior.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Pick value metric aligned to customer's own success (per seat, usage, outcomes)
2. Three tiers max; middle anchored to target segment
3. Fence by capability value, never artificial crippling
4. Grandfather existing customers through changes with clear timelines
5. Test willingness-to-pay qualitatively (van Westendorp) before quantitative rollout
6. Review packaging quarterly against sales objections and upgrade logs

## Common Pitfalls

- Pricing copied from competitors serving different segments
- Usage-based billing surprise invoices destroying trust
