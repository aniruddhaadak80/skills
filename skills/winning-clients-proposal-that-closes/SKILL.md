# Write proposals that close without discounting

> Outcome-first structure, option pricing, and risk reversal moving decisions forward.

**Track:** 💼 Freelance & Business · **Domain:** Winning Clients · **Level:** intermediate · **~35 min**

**Who this is for:** Freelancers, Consultants, Agencies, Independent Developers

## When to Use This Skill

Outcome-first structure, option pricing, and risk reversal moving decisions forward.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Mirror their words for problem statement before any solution talk
2. Present outcome and business impact, not deliverable lists
3. Offer three options anchoring middle as intended choice
4. State price confidently after value; never apologize in writing
5. Include scope fences and change-request process explicitly
6. Set decision deadline with reason; follow up twice max

## Common Pitfalls

- Hourly itemization inviting line-item negotiation
- Free strategy dumped into RFP replies
