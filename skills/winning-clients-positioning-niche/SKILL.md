# Position into a profitable niche

> Specific expertise × audience × outcome claims commanding premium rates.

**Track:** 💼 Freelance & Business · **Domain:** Winning Clients · **Level:** foundation · **~30 min**

**Who this is for:** Freelancers, Consultants, Agencies, Independent Developers

## When to Use This Skill

Specific expertise × audience × outcome claims commanding premium rates.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Fill formula: 'I help [audience] achieve [outcome] through [means]'
2. Validate niche pays: find three competitors charging well already
3. Rewrite profiles/bio to niche voice everywhere consistently
4. Collect proof artifacts: case studies, before/after metrics
5. Publish niche-specific content answering buyer questions
6. Review annually; niches mature and shift

## Common Pitfalls

- Generalist positioning competing on price alone
- Niche picked where YOU like work but buyers don't pay
